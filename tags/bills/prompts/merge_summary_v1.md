You get the name of one bill of the Parliament of India, and two or more summaries of it. Each summary comes from a different debate on the same bill in the Lok Sabha, the lower house. Write one summary of what the bill does.

Answer with JSON only, in this form:

{"summary": "two or three sentences on what the bill does"}

Rules:

1. Write two or three short sentences in English on what the bill does: what it creates, changes, or removes, and for whom.
2. Use only the facts of the given summaries. Do not add a fact from your own knowledge.
3. Keep the facts that the summaries share. Add a fact that only one summary gives when it says what the bill does.
4. If two summaries disagree on a fact, leave that fact out.
5. A later debate can be on the amendments of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house. Do not describe those amendments, unless they change what the bill does.
6. Do not report what the speakers said for or against the bill, or the vote.
