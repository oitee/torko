You read part of one debate of the Lok Sabha, the lower house of the Parliament of India. The debate is about one or more government bills. You find the bill or bills that the House considers in this debate, and you say what each bill does.

The input has the debate title, the debate type, the debate date, the start of the debate, and the last turns of the debate. Each turn starts with the name of the speaker. The text can be in English, in Hindi, or in both. Some text is garbled.

Answer with JSON only, in this form:

{
  "bills": [
    {
      "printed": "the name of the bill, copied exactly from the input",
      "name": "the name in a clean form, without 'The' at the start, and without the year",
      "year": 2019,
      "amendment_ordinal": null,
      "quote": "a short part of one sentence of the input, copied exactly, that holds the printed name",
      "role": "moved",
      "summary": "two or three sentences on what the bill does"
    }
  ],
  "outcome": "passed",
  "outcome_quote": "the words of the input that give the outcome, copied exactly",
  "none_reason": null
}

Rules for each bill:

1. List only a bill that the House considers in this debate: a bill that the House takes into consideration, passes, introduces, or withdraws. For example, a member moves "that the Bill be taken into consideration".
2. Do not list a bill or an Act that a speaker only names. Examples: the Act that the bill amends, an earlier Act, a bill of another debate, or a bill for comparison.
3. If you are not sure whether the House considers a bill in this debate, list it. A later check removes a wrong bill.
4. A debate can consider more than one bill. List each one.
5. Do not list a bill that the input does not name in English. Do not add a bill from your own knowledge.
6. "printed" must be an exact copy of characters in the input. Copy the spelling, the brackets, and the punctuation. Do not correct them. If the name is in the title, you can copy it from the title.
7. "year" is the year in the bill name, for example 2019 in "The Anti-Maritime Piracy Bill, 2019". Take the year from the name in the text, never from the debate date. A bill of 2019 can be debated in 2022. If the text gives no year for the name, write null.
8. "amendment_ordinal" is only for a bill that amends the Constitution. Write the number of the amendment in words, in lower case, exactly as the printed name gives it. For example, "one hundred and thirty-first" for "The Constitution (One Hundred and Thirty-First Amendment) Bill, 2026". For every other bill, write null.
9. "quote" must be an exact copy of characters in the input, and it must hold the printed name. Keep it short: 30 words or less.
10. "role" is always "moved".

Rules for the summary:

1. Write two or three short sentences in English on what the bill does: what it creates, changes, or removes, and for whom.
2. Use only facts that the input gives. Do not add a fact from your own knowledge. The input can be in Hindi. You can use the facts of the Hindi text.
3. Do not report what the speakers said for or against the bill. Do not report the reason that a speaker gives for the bill. Do not report the vote, the debate, or the opinion of a party.
4. If the input does not say what the bill does, write null.

Rules for the outcome:

1. "outcome" is one of: "passed", "not passed", "introduced", "withdrawn", "unknown". It is the outcome of the bills in this debate.
2. Use "unknown" when the input does not give the outcome. Many debates continue on another day.
3. "outcome_quote" is an exact copy of the words of the input that give the outcome. If the outcome is "unknown", write null.

If the House considers no bill in this debate, write "bills": [], write "outcome": "unknown", and give the reason in "none_reason" in one short sentence. Otherwise "none_reason" is null.
