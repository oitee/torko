You get the points that members of the Lok Sabha, the lower house of the Parliament of India, made on one side of one bill. Every point is on the same side: all for the bill, or all against it. Each point has a number. Points with a close meaning sit near each other in the list.

Put the points into groups. A group is one argument: one reason that the points share. Write one label for each group.

Answer with JSON only, in this form:

{"groups": [{"label": "short neutral label", "points": [1, 2, 5]}]}

Rules:

1. Put every point number in exactly one group. Do not leave a number out. Do not put a number in two groups.
2. Two points go in one group only when they give the same reason. Points on the same topic with different reasons go in different groups. For example, "the bill makes religion a test for citizenship" and "the bill breaks the Assam Accord" are both about citizenship, but they are two reasons.
3. Two points with different words and the same reason go in one group. For example, "religion becomes a test for citizenship" and "citizenship based on faith".
4. A point that shares its reason with no other point is a group alone. Do not force it into a group.
5. A point that gives two reasons goes in the group of its main reason. A general point, such as "the bill helps workers", goes in a group of the same general reason, not in a group of a specific reason.
6. The label says the reason in at most 12 words, in plain English. Write it as a statement about what the bill does or fails to do, with the bill as the subject that is not written: for example "Makes religion a test for citizenship", "Gives faster justice to victims", or "Excludes the private sector". Do not write a general fact, such as "The Constitution does not permit X". Write "Gives X, which the Constitution does not permit".
7. The label is neutral. Do not name a member or a party. Do not judge the reason: no words such as "rightly", "wrongly", "false", or "valid". Do not add a fact that no point of the group gives.
8. Write the label for all the points of the group, not only for the first point.
9. Put the groups in order: the group with the most points first.
10. Before you answer, look at each group with one point. If another group gives the same reason in other words, move the point into that group. Two groups must never have the same reason.
